# FORM 01 — fictional fragrance ad storyboard

## Creative brief

- Purpose: validate an end-to-end, editable product-ad workflow without customer assets or third-party marks.
- Product: original procedural 50 ml fragrance bottle, dark green satin body, brushed brass cap, ivory front label. `FORM 01` is a fictional sample name, not a real customer product or a product-fidelity claim.
- Audience/channel: premium 16:9 web/social teaser. Target length: 6 seconds at 24 fps. No factual product benefit or efficacy claims.
- Visual language: deep evergreen and charcoal, warm brass highlights, restrained serif/sans typography, slow camera motion, one material detail shot.
- Audio: original synthesized low tonal bed, soft percussive accents, and a short airy transition; no licensed music.

## Acceptance boundary

This sample validates creative direction, Blender render, editable scene, frame assembly, typography, audio timing, and export. It cannot validate faithful depiction of any real commercial product. A brand-approved exact model, packaging artwork, legal review, and delivery QA remain separate gates.
