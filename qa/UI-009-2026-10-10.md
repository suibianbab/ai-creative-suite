## Browser and export observations

- Reopened the saved 2-layer project. The product layer's padded bottom was Y=695, while the visible mask base was Y=671. The new control reported Y=671, confirming that it uses the visible product rather than the layer rectangle.
- Set the pedestal surface to Y=674. Applying alignment and shadow moved the layer Y from 155 to 158.42, so the visible base became Y=674. Product and mask asset references remained unchanged.
- The tuned native shadow used color `#463624`, opacity `0.50`, width scale `0.86`, depth `26`, horizontal offset `0`, vertical offset `8`. Actual 1080 × 1080 PNG export: `synthetic-pump-contact-shadow-tuned.png`, SHA-256 `C902AE1E9F52F85297FA09B76E6DDF5D3706F321765D6FE11E848487A574F911`.
- In the pedestal contact region X=460–659, Y=675–699, the tuned export averaged about 35 lower RGB levels than the UI-008 baseline and 4777 of 5000 pixels darkened by more than 5 levels. This region lies below the bottle. Visual inspection confirmed a soft shadow under the original bottle rather than a product repaint.
- Saved the project, reloaded, reopened it, and observed the two layers plus the tuned shadow parameters. In a separate unsaved transaction, one Undo removed the newly added shadow and reverted the Y alignment; one Redo restored both.

## Quality limits

The bottle no longer appears to float, but the overall light direction, shadow shape and mask edges were only checked on one synthetic opaque product. This does not approve high-end customer advertising or transparent/refraction cases. Continue with rights-cleared varied products, full-size edge QA and scene light matching.
