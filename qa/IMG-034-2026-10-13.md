# IMG-034: PhotoCraft saved selection with Qwen generated edits

## Scope

Development-only integration on the owned synthetic hat fixture `qa/fixtures/photocraft-hat-teal.png`. The original is never overwritten. Qwen `qwen-image-2.0-pro` supplies generated pixels; PhotoCraft 0.6.0 owns the saved selection, mask, native layers and `.pcraft` output.

## Cases

| Edit | Route | Result |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Brim teal to red | PhotoCraft native recolor and contour mask | Pass on this fixture; 8,288/8,289 brim samples red in the Qwen contour comparison, with the native route preferred for deterministic recolor. |
| Remove white `FORM 01` patch | Qwen generated fill + feathered saved PhotoCraft rectangle | Functional pass: label removed, outside selection unchanged, two-layer source reopened. Reconstructed fabric appears smoother than the original; professional retouch quality remains pending. |
| Gray background to warm beige | Qwen generation + saved PhotoCraft subject inverse selection | Functional pass: generated background imported in a two-layer source. Visual fail: old gray shadow remains along cap edge and under brim. |

Additional native PhotoCraft selection attempts used `select.object` and `select.colorRange` subtraction for neutral shadow pixels before inversion. Both still left a visible gray/brown band below the cap. The shadow transition needs manual mask work or a different background compositing source; this test does not mark it as passed.

The bridge's mask brush now accepts any selected PhotoCraft layer with a native mask, including the generated Smart Object. A brush stroke on that real Qwen layer saved and reopened correctly. A scripted broad stroke across the old shadow created additional visible artifacts, so the brushed candidate also fails visual review. It is retained as a diagnostic, not the chosen result.
