## Interpretation

This check is a cue for human review. It deliberately does not auto-recolor the bottle from statistics: intrinsic material color and lighting cannot be distinguished reliably from one product photograph and one background plate. It also does not validate transparent/refractive products, real color management, or final advertising quality.
